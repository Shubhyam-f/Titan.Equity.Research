# Titan Company Limited (NSE: TITAN) - Fundamental Equity Research & DCF Valuation

An end-to-end fundamental equity research report and bottom-up Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) valuation model for Titan Company Limited, reverse-engineering market sentiment to determine intrinsic value.

## 📊 Project Highlights
* **Recommendation:** ACCUMULATE 
* **Current Market Price:** ~₹4,705
* **Base Case Intrinsic Value:** ₹652 per share (Strict 5-Year Horizon)
* **Market-Implied Pricing:** To justify the current ₹4,700+ price, the market is pricing in an uninterrupted 15-year hyper-growth runway (15% CAGR) at a highly generous ~10% WACC and 25% ROIC.

## 🛠️ Methodology & Technical Stack
* **Financial Modeling (Excel):** Constructed a fully integrated fundamental unlevered Free Cash Flow to the Firm (FCFF) model.
* **Cost of Capital (Python/Excel):** Calculated a strict 12.37% WACC, utilizing a bottom-up peer median beta and a 5-year weekly regression against the NIFTY 50.
* **Segment Forecasting:** Built weighted top-line growth (15.42% base case) across Jewellery (Tanishq, Zoya, Mia, CaratLane), Watches, EyeCare, and Emerging Businesses.
* **Capital Structure Bridge:** Executed enterprise-to-equity bridge accounting for ₹30,621.0 crore in debt (including short-term metal gold-on-loan funding) against non-operating strategic investments.

## 💡 Investment Thesis Summary
While a strict 5-year fundamental DCF suggests the stock is currently priced for perfection, Titan's operational momentum and structural transition justify the premium multiple. The FY26 acquisition of Damas Jewellery LLC provides a massive catalyst, opening direct access to the Arab luxury consumer and extending Titan's growth runway well beyond domestic borders. Combined with a 36% dividend hike demonstrating immense earnings quality, Titan remains a premier global lifestyle compounding engine for long-term capital appreciation.
